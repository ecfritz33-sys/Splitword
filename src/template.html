How to play

Every letter becomes its place in the alphabet, A=1 to Z=26, and the numbers are run together. Your job is to cut them apart again.

81521195 cut as 8 | 15 | 21 | 19 | 5 spells HOUSE.

  • 1
    Tap between two digits to cut. Tap the same spot again to remove the cut.
  • 2
    Watch for the traps. 15 could be O, or A then E.
  • 3
    A 0 never starts a letter. It always belongs to 10 (J) or 20 (T).
  • 4
    Press Check any time. It tells you how many cuts are right, but each wrong check adds 10 seconds.
  • 5
    Your score is your time and the number of cuts you made. Fewer is better.

Three new puzzles arrive every day at midnight: Kid shows the letters as you cut, Regular hides one digit and gives you 3 peeks, and Expert hides two digits with no help.
